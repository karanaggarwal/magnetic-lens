Magnetic Dome

A 3D heat-map of the magnetic field around you. Point the phone in every direction and slowly sweep — disturbed directions glow, from blue to red, painted onto a sphere you can look around.

Uses camera + motion sensors. Nothing leaves your device. Hold still 2s after starting to set the baseline. On iPhone this maps compass deflection; on Android it uses real µT.
